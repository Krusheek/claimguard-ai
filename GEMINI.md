# Agent Directives

## Continuous Improvement Protocol
Every time you make modifications to this codebase, before concluding your task, you must:
1. **Self-Reflect:** Explicitly ask yourself if the changes you just made are optimal and if any edge cases were overlooked.
2. **Learn:** Identify at least one technical or architectural takeaway from the current task.
3. **Improve:** Apply that learning immediately to any subsequent changes, and state your learning in your final response to the user.
